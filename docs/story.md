# 🗡️ The Shattered Crown - Story & Narrative

## Chapter 1 — The Red Glow

Morning came quietly to the kingdom of Eldoria.

The sun rose over green hills and ancient stone walls, while the distant mountains disappeared into a layer of mist. Birds flew above the rooftops of a small village at the edge of the kingdom.

The village was called Oakrest.

It was a peaceful place. Farmers worked the fields, merchants opened their shops, and children ran through the narrow streets.

But beyond the village stood an enormous forest.

The Whispering Woods.

Nobody went deep into the forest anymore.

Old stories claimed that something had lived there long before the kingdom was founded.

Most people believed those stories were nothing more than legends.

Until that morning.

At the edge of the forest, something moved.

A single black leaf fell from a tree.

Then another.

Then hundreds.

The wind suddenly stopped.

For several seconds, the entire forest became completely silent.

Then—

**BOOM.**

A deep sound echoed through the trees.

Back in Oakrest, everyone stopped what they were doing.

A farmer dropped his shovel.

A merchant stepped outside his shop.

The village guards immediately reached for their swords.

And somewhere inside the forest, a strange red glow appeared between the trees.

Nobody knew what had caused it.

But whatever had awakened...

it was coming.

## Chapter 1 Full Blueprint — The Red Glow

### Chapter Purpose
Chapter 1 accomplishes five things:
1. Introduce **Oakrest** and its people
2. Teach mechanics: movement, interaction, gathering, combat, pacification, alchemy, and crafting
3. Establish the mystery of the **Whispering Woods**
4. Let the player experience the first consequences of the red glow
5. End with a strong reason to continue into Chapter 2

### Event Flow

**Event 1:** Title Screen → New Game
**Event 2:** Player's Home (interaction tutorial)
**Event 3:** First Walk Through Oakrest (movement tutorial)
**Event 4:** The First Strange Sign (black leaf)
**Event 5:** Village Square → The BOOM moment
**Event 6-7:** First Enemy - Wild Boar (combat + pacify tutorial)
**Event 8:** Elder's Warning → Quest: The Forest's Edge
**Event 9:** Herbalist - First Gathering
**Event 10:** Alchemy Introduction
**Event 11:** Crafting Introduction
**Event 12:** Leaving Oakrest
**Event 13:** Whispering Woods Outer Edge
**Event 14:** First Forest Encounter (Vine Lurker)
**Event 15:** The Broken Shrine
**Event 16:** First Crafting Materials
**Event 17:** Path Splits (crossroads)
**Event 18:** Optional Side Event (lost animal)
**Event 19:** The Red Mist
**Event 20:** Corrupted Boar (serious combat)
**Event 21:** Unknown Fragment discovered
**Event 22:** Return to Oakrest - Elder's concern
**Event 23:** Evening in Oakrest (atmosphere shift)
**Event 24:** Optional Night Preparation
**Event 25:** The Second BOOM (night falls)
**Event 26:** Village Defense (multiple creatures)
**Event 27:** Final Creature (pacify choice)
**Event 28:** Forest Answers (massive silhouette)
**Event 29:** Elder's Revelation
**Event 30:** Chapter Complete

### Mechanics Progression
Movement → Exploration → Gathering → Alchemy → Crafting → Combat → Pacify → Forest Exploration → Stronger Enemies → Pacify vs. Defeat Choices → Village Defense → Chapter Mystery

### Central Mystery
By chapter end, player knows:
1. Something caused the red glow
2. Creatures are being affected by it
3. The Elder knows more than revealed

But the player does NOT yet know what the red glow actually is.
